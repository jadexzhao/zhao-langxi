Digital Humans as useful business interfaces

Looking human is not the product. I am building toward Digital Humans that help people on an ordinary day ... including for small organisations that cannot afford a face that fails on the floor. There is no single right way. Trust, disclosure, and keeping people are still open questions, not a finished launch. Say the true thing. Be here. Highlight reels are not the story. You are not behind if the path changed. Show up for the people who actually use the system.
