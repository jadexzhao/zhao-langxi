Digital Humans as a question

Looking human is not the product. Respect is.

I am working on Digital Humans that might help on an ordinary day ... including for small organisations that cannot afford a face that fails on the floor. Kindness without accountability is soft. Accountability without kindness is brittle. Trust, disclosure, and keeping people are still open questions, not a finished launch.

Say the true thing. Be here. Highlight reels are not the story. You are not behind if the path changed. Show up for the people who actually use the system.
