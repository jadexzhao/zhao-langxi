What this door is for

赵郎溪 is the essay room for the Digital Humans question I am carrying: useful business interfaces for small organisations, trust on an ordinary day, disclosure that people can actually read. It foreshadows the project. It does not pretend the product is finished.

Roles

Primary: Digital Humans Project Lead at Luddy (February 2025 to present). Digital Humans are systems that look and talk like a person. On an ordinary day you tell the guest it is not a person, check the answer, and hand off to a human. Looking human is not the product.

On Handshake I am an influencer for early talent. Short posts and videos for the student feed. The feed rewards a polished profile and punishes a voice that feels performed. If the person on the page cannot be trusted, students leave. Hiring managers do too. Same question as Digital Humans, on a feed instead of a kiosk.

On campus I am also Program Assistant, Office of Student Life, for HHSP (Hudson & Holland Scholars Program). I am a Hudson & Holland Platinum Scholar and a first-generation college student.

Where the instinct started

I grew up in a family Chinese restaurant in central Indiana. Floor, till, kitchen. Friday night. After COVID I rebuilt the website and social. That path led into Informatics, Business and Society, at IU Luddy.

Ships that trained the instinct

I build databases, pipelines, internal tools, and websites staff can maintain. Through Serve IT I have shipped for partners without a developer on call, including Middle Way House, City of Bloomington, Indiana Phenology Lab, and the Bloomington Chamber. This term: accessibility assessment for the Town of Nashville. The same handoff question shows up there: will this still work after the student team leaves?

After May 2027

After May 2027 I am looking for software engineering close to databases, internal systems, and ETL (extract, transform, load). Longer term I want products of my own, and eventually a duck farm.
